Owner decision after reviewing a known local build

The integration exposes miner-accept-risk --sha256 HASH through raven.py.
The Windows supervisor exposes -Action AcceptRisk -ExpectedSha256 HASH.
This records owner risk acceptance, not remediation or a false-positive verdict.
It recognizes only the reviewed Wacatac B/C/H and Caypnamer classifications and
exact xmrig.exe paths. The receipt binds the executable SHA and incident keys.
New classifications, resources, incident identities/status or modified files
remain blocked. Existing checks for unrelated incidents are retained.

No Defender preferences, exclusions, quarantine state or processes are changed
by acceptance. Preparation can replace an old installation, but execution still
requires source/installed hash agreement and the ordinary scan, GPU library,
pool validation and thermal gates. Rebuilding does not inherit the old consent.
Run as a standard user. The installer scanner may request UAC; the miner does not.

check-risk-consent.ps1 exercises 15 inert-fixture scenarios, including rollback,
changed incidents, wrong names/resources, file modification, old installations
and disabled Defender. It never changes Defender or starts a miner.
